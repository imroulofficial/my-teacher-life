Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Red Pen Studio ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the mobile game My Teacher Life (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • The App is 100% offline. The game is played on your device and does not need an internet connection.
  • We do not collect any personal data. We have no servers and receive no information about you.
  • No account creation, no sign-in, no analytics, and no tracking SDKs.
  • The only third-party service is Google AdMob, used to show optional Rewarded Video ads that you choose to watch. There are no banner ads and no interstitial (pop-up) ads.

2. Information we collect

Red Pen Studio does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details.

3. Game data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, school upgrades, settings and the name you give your teacher character) is saved only on your device using the device's local storage (LocalStorage/IndexedDB). This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset all progress in the game, by clearing the App's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the App.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob Rewarded Video ads only. A rewarded video is shown only when you tap a button to watch it in exchange for an in-game reward (for example, extra coins). Ads need an internet connection; if you are offline, no ads are requested and the game still works.

When an ad is requested, Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version);
  • Ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was viewed or completed).

Google uses this information to serve, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. This processing is performed by Google, not by Red Pen Studio, and is governed by Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses cookies and identifiers in advertising

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads depending on your device).

5. Third-party services

The only third-party service integrated in the App is:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) — rewarded video advertising. Privacy Policy

The App does not include any analytics, crash-reporting, social media or tracking SDKs.

6. Children's privacy (COPPA)

The App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country). Red Pen Studio itself collects no personal information from any user. Advertising in the App is provided by Google AdMob under Google's policies for families and children where applicable. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help address your concern.

7. Users in the EU, EEA and UK (GDPR)

Red Pen Studio does not process your personal data. Ad-related processing is carried out by Google AdMob. Where required by law, the App shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are served, so you can give or refuse consent. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Under the GDPR you have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Because we hold no personal data about you, requests concerning ad data should be directed to Google via Google's privacy tools. You may also contact us with any question.

8. California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

Red Pen Studio does not sell or share your personal information, and does not collect personal information as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act. Google AdMob may process device identifiers for advertising as described above. California residents may opt out of personalized advertising using the device settings described in section 4 and may exercise their rights with Google directly. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any privacy right.

9. Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device and is kept until you reset it, clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Because we do not receive any of your data, we do not retain it on any server.

10. Permissions used by the App

  • Internet / network state — only to load optional reward videos from Google AdMob.
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob for ad serving.
  • Vibration — for optional haptic feedback during gameplay (can be turned off in Settings).

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
